Plan an eight-second reel for your own account

In the Brush Nyang example, the cat’s jump towards the camera becomes the signal to start a brushing timer. The timings below are a worksheet for a new reel.

Choose the last frame first. For an app, write down the one feature a viewer should remember. For a character, choose something only that character would do.

Three ways to use the same jump
1. Start signal. The cat jumps just as a thumb hits Start. Cut to the ticking timer.
2. Mistaken identity. Match the cat’s face to an illustrated face in the same pose. Pull back to reveal the character on a sticker.
3. Running joke. The cat keeps landing in the wrong place. On the final jump, it lands exactly where it belongs. For a notes app, the last landing could put a scattered idea neatly into its list.
